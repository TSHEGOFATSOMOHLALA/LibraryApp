Welcome to the Vet Office Management System

This system helps veterinary clinics manage owners and their patients efficiently. You can use the system to maintain records of pet owners and the animals registered with the clinic.

System Features

  • Maintain a list of pet owners.
  • Register and manage animal patients.
  • Update owner and patient information.
  • Remove records that are no longer needed.

Getting Started

Use the navigation menu above to view the available records in the system.

  • Owners – View, add, edit, or delete owner information.
  • Patients – View, add, edit, or delete animal patient records.

The Vet Office Management System is a simple web application built with the Django framework. It demonstrates how databases and web applications can be used together to manage information in a veterinary practice.
